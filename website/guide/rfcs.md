# RFCs

Protocol changes are proposed as RFCs in the
[`rfcs/`](https://github.com/BlockSiFr/ttp-protocol/tree/main/rfcs) directory.
Anything that touches the grammar, semantics, schemas, or trust model needs one.

## Process

1. Open a **Protocol RFC** discussion to socialize the idea.
2. Draft an RFC from `rfcs/0000-template.md`.
3. Open a PR; mark it `status: needs-rfc` until accepted.
4. On acceptance, the change lands with tests and docs.
